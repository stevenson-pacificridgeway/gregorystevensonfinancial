Questions

Straight answers

What is an indexed annuity?

It's a contract with an insurance company that lets your money grow based on a market index, while protecting your principal from market losses. You share in a portion of the gains in good years and lose nothing to the market in down years.

Can you lose money in an indexed annuity?

Not from a market downturn — your principal is protected. The things to understand are fees on any optional riders and surrender charges if you withdraw more than allowed in the early years.

How much income will $500,000 generate?

A common starting estimate is about $20,000 a year using the 4% rule. A guaranteed-income annuity can often pay more and keeps paying for life, so you can't outlive the income. See the full breakdown →
